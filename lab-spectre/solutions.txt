Discussion Question 5: Describe your strategy to extend the speculation window of the target
branch in the victim.
We can extend the speculation window of the target branch by evicting the values in the cache after training the branch predictor. This evicts the part3_limit value, causing the speculation window to widen since the CPU then has to load the value from memory.
